Hello! I’m Anup Paudel, a Computer Science student fascinated by the intersection of computational logic and human experience.

Originally from the picturesque landscapes of Nepal, I am currently pursuing my higher education in the United States. My passion for digital creation began with coding fundamentals and quickly expanded into UI/UX design when I realized that the best technology is the one that feels effortless to use.

Rather than viewing engineering and aesthetics as separate disciplines, I treat them as complementary sides of problem-solving. My goal is to build digital products that are accessible, performant, visually engaging, and deeply meaningful to their users.
